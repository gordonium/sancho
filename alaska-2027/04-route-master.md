# Route Master — v3 (D+n)

*v1/v2 archived in `archive/04-route-master-v1-v2.md`.*

## How to read this
- **Front half is relative:** D+0 = the day you leave Loveland. Overnight is at the end of that day.
- **Back half is absolute:** pinned by the moon (aurora Aug 5–10) and home by Aug 23.
- **They meet in Tok.** Target: **arrive Tok on D+56 = Aug 2**, leaving 2 days of banked slack before Aug 4.
- **So full scope → depart ~June 7.** Every cut in the dial (§4) moves departure later.

Tags: **[BOOK]** reserve ahead (sells out) · **[FC]** first-come / sketch only ·
**[FLOAT]** vibes day — where lingering goes, and first to cut ·
⛽ fuel note · 💧 water/dump · 🛒 real grocery · 📵 call-dark (unreachable)

Mileage is approximate. Campground reservability to be verified at booking time.
Calls happen anywhere — pull over, deploy Starlink, keep rolling (`12`).

Separately, **[FLOAT] days** (Jasper, Dawson, Byers Lake, Talkeetna, Homer, Valdez, McCarthy) are
where lingering happens. Stay longer somewhere and a float day elsewhere pays for it.
Burn through all seven and you're into the 2 banked slack days — don't go past those.
