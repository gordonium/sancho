## 1. AURORA — the locked window

### Moon (verified)
**New moon: Monday, August 2, 2027.** Verifiable independently: Aug 2, 2027 is the
total solar eclipse over North Africa (6m23s, longest land totality of the century).
Solar eclipses only occur at new moon.

| Phase | Date |
|---|---|
| **New moon** | **Aug 2** |
| First quarter | Aug 9 |
| **Full moon** | **Aug 17** ⚠ wrecks mid-August |
| Next new moon | Aug 31 |

### Darkness math
At solar midnight, sun depth below horizon = solar declination − (90° − latitude).
Aurora needs roughly −12° or lower.

**Fairbanks (64.8°N) is hopeless in this window** — only reaches −8° in early August,
−12° around Aug 18. Aurora "season" starting Aug 21 is real astronomy, not marketing.

**In August, driving SOUTH gets you darker nights** while staying under the auroral
oval. The Yukon/northern BC corridor is the answer.

### ★ The block: Aug 5–10, Yukon → northern BC
Each night darker than the last — calendar advancing AND latitude dropping.
Moon cooperates throughout (waxing from new on Aug 2).

| Night | Location | Lat | Sun at midnight | Moon sets |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aug 5 | Destruction Bay, YT | 61.2°N | −12.1° | ~10:00 pm |
| Aug 6 | Whitehorse, YT | 60.7°N | −12.8° | ~10:45 pm |
| Aug 7 | Teslin, YT | 60.2°N | −13.5° | ~11:30 pm |
| Aug 8 | Watson Lake, YT | 60.1°N | −13.8° | ~12:15 am |
| **Aug 9** | **Dease Lake, BC** | **58.4°N** | **−15.8°** | ~1:00 am |
| **Aug 10** | **Iskut/Tatogga, BC** | **57.8°N** | **−16.6°** | ~1:45 am |

Plan short driving days Aug 5–10 — up at 1 am every night.
Forecast tools: NOAA SWPC Ovation, UAF Geophysical Institute. Kp 3 is enough here.

**This block does not move.** Everything before it is adjustable; nothing after it
has slack.

## 2. TEMPERATURE

Preference: overnight lows **below 60°F** (40s and high 30s welcome).
Over 60 overnight is the failure condition. Days over mid-80s unpleasant.
**Roof AC available on shore power** → heat zones need full-hookup sites.

### Performance against climate normals
~62 of 70 nights below 60°F. June 21 – Aug 14 is essentially never uncomfortable.

### ⚠ Heat zones — book full hookups with reliable 30A
| Zone | Dates | Normals | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Badlands SD | ~Jun 18 | 87 / 59 | Waypoint only now — drive through |
| **Fairbanks** | Jul 15–19 | 72 / 52, **spikes to 88** | Hit 90°F on Jul 4, 2019. Staying with people — likely indoors. |
| **Cache Creek, BC** | ~Aug 15 | **84 / 56** | Thompson Valley, BC's hot spot. Can hit 100. |
| **Methow Valley, WA** | ~Aug 17 | **85 / 52** | Winthrop |
| **Ellensburg, WA** | Aug 19–20 | **86 / 55** | 2 nights booked — get hookups |
| **Boise / Snake River Plain** | Aug 21 | **92 / 63** | **Worst day of the trip.** Consider Baker City or Ontario OR instead (higher, cooler). |
| Craters of the Moon | Aug 22 | 88 / 55 | Early start, park by 2 pm |
| Home, Loveland | Aug 23 | 87 / 57 | Arrival day |

### Heat tactic
For the Aug 15–23 run: **roll at 6 am, parked by 2 pm.** Drive the cool hours, sit out
the hot ones on shore power.

### ⚠ Wildfire smoke
Peak late June – mid August, Interior Alaska and BC. Most likely thing to degrade both
comfort and aurora nights. No cabin filter in the rig — see `01-rig.md` §6.
Monitor PurpleAir + AirNow and be willing to reroute.

## 3. FUEL — 160 mi reliable / 220 mi max

Two 5-gal cans ≈ 100 extra miles. Carry them full only on the flagged stretches.

### ⚠ Genuine problems
| Stretch | Gap | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Tatogga/Iskut → Bell II** (Cassiar 37) | **~140 mi** | The one that bites. Hills, no services. Cans full. |
| **Watson Lake → Teslin** (Alaska Hwy) | **~160 mi** | Rancheria + Swift River are seasonal/intermittent. Don't count on them. |

### Tight but fine
Fort Nelson → Toad River 120 · Beaver Creek → Tok 110 · Dawson City → Chicken 108
· Glennallen → Tok 139 (fuel at Chistochina/Slana)

### ⚠ Off-limits
**Dalton Hwy: Coldfoot → Deadhorse = 240 mi.** Impossible. Arctic Circle day tour
as a passenger instead — also spares the windshield and tires.

### Operating rule
North of Dawson Creek, **fill at every opportunity above half a tank.** Not a third.
Many stations close at 8–9 pm, some are cash-only, some are closed for the season
without notice. Get **The Milepost 2027** — it lists every stop with exact distances.

# Session 3 update (2026-09-24) — breakdown risk reframed

Gordon: *"Luckily while waiting for a tow truck, I'm in my house with Starlink and
probably a great view."*

**Correct, and it changes the framing.** A breakdown in this rig is not a safety
event — he has shelter, power, water, food, and comms. **It is a schedule event.**

So the spares box isn't buying safety. It's buying days. And **there is exactly one
window where days are non-fungible: the aurora block, Aug 5–10.** It cannot slip —
after Aug 10 he's driving south into brighter latitudes and toward a hard Aug 23.

### The real insurance is slack, not parts
| Breakdown date | Cost |
|---|---|
| June, anywhere | Comfort and a few plans. Absorbed. |
| July, Alaska | Trims sightseeing. Annoying, survivable. |
| **Jul 28 – Aug 4** | ⚠ **Could cost the entire aurora goal.** |
| After Aug 11 | Eats Ellensburg or forces a harder run home. |

**Design rule: arrive in Tok by Aug 4 with at least two days of unused slack.**
Front-load the buffer into late July so a 3-day parts wait in Glennallen doesn't
end the trip's primary objective. That buffer is worth more than anything in the tote.
