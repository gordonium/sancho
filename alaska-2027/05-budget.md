# Budget — SOLO (v2)

70 days, ~10,000 miles, 2027 dollars. **Revised for solo travel** — earlier versions
assumed two people, which roughly doubled the sightseeing line.

## Fuel assumptions
- 5VZ-FE swap + 55–60 mph on state highways → **estimate 14 mpg** (up from 13
  with the 3VZ). Sunraders run 12–16; the taller gearing question could move this.
- ~10,000 mi ÷ 14 = **~715 gallons**
- Pricing: lower-48 ~$3.70/gal · Canada ~$4.80/gal (CAD $1.65/L) ·
  Alaska & remote Yukon/BC ~$5.10–6.50/gal
