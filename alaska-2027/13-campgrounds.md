# Campgrounds & Overnights — v2 (researched 2026-09-24)

*v1 (from memory) archived. This version is checked against official and operator sources.*

**About the numbers:** 2027 rates and openings aren't published yet. Every rate here is
the **latest published figure** — marked (2026) where the source dated it, (published)
where it didn't. **All prices in USD** — Canadian rates converted at 1 CAD ≈ $0.71 (Sept 2026). Dates assume a **June 7 departure**.
Hookups: FHU = full · E/W = electric + water · — = none.

## Heat — the nights are almost never the problem
By the overnight-over-60°F rule, no night fails once Boise is avoided. Heat is an
afternoon issue → electric at Hope (Aug 14), Baker City (Aug 21), Arco (Aug 22).
Ellensburg: staying in the house. Tacoma: guest room.

### D+35–38 · Jul 12–15 · Kesugi Ridge Sampler ⚠
**Byers Lake, Denali View North and K'esugi Ken were all closed in 2026.** Byers takes
Reserve America bookings ~7 months ahead *when open*.
- **If Byers opens in 2027:** plan as written — book ~Dec 12 for Jul 12.
- **If not:** sleep D+35 at the nearest open campground (Denali View South and Lower
  Troublesome Creek were open in 2026), drive to Little Coal Creek trailhead (Mile 163.9)
  on D+36, rig stays at the trailhead one night, then the float day moves to Talkeetna.
