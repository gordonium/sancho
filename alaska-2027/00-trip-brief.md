# Trip Brief

## The trip
Solo road trip, Loveland CO → Saskatoon SK → Alaska (to Fairbanks and beyond)
→ back through BC and Washington → Loveland. Roughly 70 days, ~10,000 miles.

## Hard dates
| Date | Constraint |

## Must-haves
- **Northern lights.** Primary emotional goal.
- **Orcas.** Particular interest.
- Saskatoon — 2 nights min (visiting people)
- Fairbanks — 2 nights min, planning 4 (visiting people)
- Tacoma WA — 1 night
- Ellensburg WA — 2 nights min
- Denali NP, Kenai Fjords NP, Wrangell–St. Elias NP
- Ocean views and **coastal storms** — willing to reroute for coastal highways

## Already visited — waypoint only, no destination time needed
Devils Tower NM, Wind Cave NP, Badlands NP, Theodore Roosevelt NP

## Temperature preference
- Overnight lows **over 60°F are the problem.** Below 60 is good.
- 40s fine. High 30s fine.
- Daytime over mid-80s unpleasant.
- **With shore power he has roof AC** → hot sections are survivable IF booked
  into full-hookup sites with reliable 30A. Plan cushy campgrounds for heat zones.

## Travel style — important for itinerary design
- **Solo.**
- "This isn't a sprint, it's a mosey." But capable: drove ATL→Loveland in 2 days
  with one overnight in St. Louis (~700 mi/day). 400–500 mi/day is easy.
- **Prefers state highways over interstates.**
- Comfortable max ~65 mph; **55–60 preferred.**
- **Likes to linger where it feels good** → schedule must not feel relentless.
  Need unscheduled float days.
- **Gets bored easily.** After ~1 day a static attraction is finished.
  Grand Canyon analogy: "just a big hole in the ground with nothing to do
  if you're not multi-day hiking below the rim."
  → Favor places with LAYERED activity: towns, hot springs, boats, people,
    weird history, things to do. Avoid 3-night stays at scenic overlooks.

# Session 2 revisions (2026-09-24)

## Departure: ~June 7, 2027 or earlier ✓
Priority stated: **"I'd rather leave earlier and have margin than be tight on days
coming home."** Trip length grows to ~78 days.

## Coastal package: Valdez + Skagway/Haines + orca day ✓
**Olympic NP dropped**, to protect the run home. Prince Rupert dropped.
Coastal storms deprioritized — *"I can catch those elsewhere and other times."*
Net: +6 days of coastal content.

## ⚠ Ellensburg expands — up to a full week
Decompress with **Uncle Jeff & Aunt Sara**. Anywhere from 2 to 7 nights.
**Family, not a reservation** → this is the perfect float buffer. Decide on the road.

## Travel style — correction to earlier assumption
> *"Beautiful nothing to do is actually also good. I can sit in a place for two days
> and stare at it. Just depends on the vibes — hard to predict."*

**This supersedes the earlier linger-worthy / one-and-done classification.** Don't
pre-sort destinations by how much there is to do. Instead:

**Build the minimum viable schedule and leave the maximum number of nights unbooked.**

Vibes are the scheduling algorithm and they can't be predicted in September 2026.
The planning job is to protect optionality, not to fill days. The Yukon Government
first-come campground system becomes central rather than incidental.

Still true: **don't book 4 nights anywhere on the assumption it'll be great.**
Book 1–2, leave room to extend.
