## Phase 2 · Early April 2027 · the decision, before anything comes apart
Once the 3VZ is out, you're committed. Decide with:
- 7,000 miles of 3VZ data from the winter loop
- Doug's availability, or a pro shop's
- **A quote on custom driveshaft lead time** (typically days to ~2 weeks)
- **A ToyOnlySwaps diagnostic slot for late May** (the Eugene shakedown's safety net)
- The departure dial (`04` §4)

**Rough timing on the swap path:** start mid-April → swap 3–5 weeks → done ~May 10–20 →
Eugene round trip ~2,600 mi ≈ 7–8 days → back ~late May. That works for June 7 if the swap
goes smoothly; if it runs long, the dial absorbs it with a later departure.

**No-swap path:** April goes to suspension + service, and you build the 3VZ spares kit.

## Phase 3 · Mid-April → late May · swap path
1. **Suspension rebuild to final ride height**, at the expected loaded weight — first,
   because the driveshaft measurement depends on it
2. Pull the 3VZ; install 5VZ + transmission, cooling, harness/ECU
   → **note the ECU/harness generation** (sets which OBD reader to carry)
3. Fit the exhaust; check clearance to floor, tanks, wiring, and propane lines
4. **Measure and order the driveshaft** — only after 1 and 2
5. First drives; re-torque everything
6. **Hard local loop:** loaded, hot day, Trail Ridge / Berthoud / Poudre Canyon
7. **Eugene run** (~1,300 mi each way), ToyOnlySwaps diagnostic slot booked ahead
8. Build the 5VZ spares kit (`02` §B)
