## No reservations needed — the flexibility buffer
- **Yukon Government campgrounds** — all first-come, first-served, ~USD $15,
  firewood included, consistently excellent. These are what make lingering possible.
- Most BC Provincial Parks outside Liard
- Dispersed camping on BLM / National Forest in the lower 48
- **Yukon River ferry at Dawson City — free, no booking**

## Strategy note
Book the **anchors** (things that sell out, things with hard dates) and deliberately
leave the **connectors** unbooked. The Yukon Government campground system is the
release valve: it means the Aug 5–10 aurora block can flex by a day for weather
without cascading into a reservation problem.
