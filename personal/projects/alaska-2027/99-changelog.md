  window where it really hurts is before the Aug 5–10 aurora block.
  → **New design rule: reach Tok by Aug 4 with two days of unused slack.**
  Slack is better insurance than parts.

**★ Highest-value open item: a bigger fuel tank**
Reported stock Sunrader tanks range 13 / 21 / 23 / 26 gal across years; owners have
gone 13 → 23. Moving from ~17 to 26 gal takes reliable range from **160 to ~250 mi**,
which **eliminates the Cassiar 140-mi gap** and retires the jerry cans.
**Check the stripped Seabreeze's tank first — it may be a free upgrade.**

**Inverter resolved to a number**
Air fryer is 13–18% of the bank per use — excellent. Space heater is ~1.7 hrs — poor
off-grid, good on shore power to save propane. Propane holds **46× the heat energy** of
the full battery bank, so never heat from the battery. **2000W = ~185A, 3000W = ~280A;
the BMS continuous rating decides which is actually available.**

**Confirmed**
- Seabreeze stripped to the frame, leaning donor. Harness + ECU come with the engine,
  which answers the OBD-generation question.
- "Book only what sells out, sketch the rest" adopted as the planning method.
