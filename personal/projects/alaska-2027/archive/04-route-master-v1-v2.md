# Route Master

**Status: v1 route below is sound but predates the coastal reroute and the
"linger/bores easily" design principles. Coastal decisions pending — see §3.**

---

## 1. Design principles (from `00-trip-brief.md`)

1. **The Aug 5–10 aurora block does not move.** Plan backward from it.
2. **State highways over interstates.** Slower is fine; 55–60 mph preferred.
3. **200–350 mi is a comfortable day**; 400–500 is available when the road is
   boring and he wants to cover ground. Don't schedule 500 as routine.
4. **Anchor days vs. float days.** Anchor = booked, fixed. Float = unbooked,
   absorbs lingering and weather. Target **8–10 float days** in the 70.
5. **Layered activity over scenery alone.** One night at a viewpoint, two to four
   nights where there's a town, boats, history, hot springs, or people.
6. **Heat zones get full hookups** (AC on shore power).

### Linger-worthy vs. one-and-done
Critical given the stated boredom threshold.

## 3. ⚠ COASTAL REROUTE — candidates, ranked

Gordon: *"I'd be happy to reroute to maximize coastal highways. I love ocean views
and coastal storms."*

### Candidate adds

| # | Add | Days | Miles | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Valdez** — Richardson Hwy, Thompson Pass, Worthington Glacier, Columbia Glacier cruise, Prince William Sound | +2 | +230 | ★★★ Best value. Slots between Kenai and Wrangell–St. Elias with almost no disruption. |
| **2** | **Skagway + Haines loop** — Klondike Hwy from Whitehorse to Skagway (Klondike Gold Rush NHP, White Pass railway), AMHS ferry to Haines, Haines Hwy back to Haines Junction | +3 | +290 | ★★★ Two of the great drives on the continent. Adds a national historical park. Deep fjord scenery. |
| **3** | **Olympic Peninsula loop** — Hurricane Ridge, Hoh Rain Forest, Ruby Beach, Rialto, Lake Quinault | +3 | +330 | ★★★ Adds Olympic NP. Best sustained ocean views of the trip. |
| **4** | **Prince Rupert, BC** — west from Kitwanga on the Yellowhead. North Pacific Cannery, Khutzeymateen grizzly sanctuary by boat | +2 | +290 | ★★ **Best coastal-storm bet on the route** — 100+ in/yr of rain. Mid-August is still shoulder season for storms though. |
| 5 | **AMHS Inside Passage** — Haines/Skagway → Prince Rupert with the rig aboard | +3 | −900 | ★ Spectacular, but ~$1,200–1,800 and it **destroys the Aug 5–10 aurora block.** Recommend no. |

### ★ The orca add (not optional, in my view)
**San Juan Islands tour from Anacortes or Friday Harbor, Aug 16–17.** The Sumas
border crossing puts him **20 miles from Anacortes.** Bigg's/transient orcas in the
Salish Sea in August are the most reliable orca viewing in North America — the
fish-eating Southern Residents are down to ~73 animals and frequently absent, while
the mammal-eating transients are increasing and consistently seen. Book an operator
that publishes sighting rates. Humpbacks, minke, Steller sea lions, Dall's porpoise
on the same boat. **+1 day, +40 mi, $135.**

### The arithmetic
- Days freed by dropping Dakota park time: **~5**
- Candidates 1+2+3 plus the orca day: **+9 days**
- Adding Prince Rupert: **+11 days**

**Shortfall: 4–6 days.** Three ways to close it:
1. **Depart ~Jun 11 instead of Jun 15** (+4) — cleanest, contingent on Loveland heat
2. Drop Prince Albert NP (+1) and trim Denali to 2 nights (already assumed)
3. Drop Prince Rupert (candidate 4) — the most expendable

**Recommended package: 1 + 2 + 3 + orca day, departing ~Jun 11.**
Gets Valdez, Skagway/Haines, Olympic NP, and the orcas. Drops Prince Rupert.
Net effect on the master plan: +9 days of coastal content, absorbed by a 4-day
earlier departure and the 5 days freed from the Dakotas.

### Where each add slots
| Add | Insert point | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Skagway/Haines | Leg 3, after Whitehorse (~Jul 8–10) | Replaces the plain Haines Junction approach. Ferry Skagway→Haines, then Haines Hwy north. |
| Valdez | Leg 7/8 hinge (~Aug 1–2) | Glennallen → Valdez → back, or Valdez → Whittier ferry |
| Olympic NP | Leg 11, after the orca day (~Aug 17–19) | Port Townsend ferry in, loop US-101, exit to Tacoma |
| Orca day | Leg 11 start (~Aug 16) | Anacortes, immediately after Sumas |

### Coastal-storm reality check
August is the **driest** month on the Washington and BC coasts. Real Pacific storms
start arriving late September — after the tour begins. Prince Rupert and the Olympic
coast are the best shots, but expect fog, drizzle, and big swell rather than a proper
blow. Worth saying out loud so it isn't a disappointment.
