## Rolling-window formula (if the departure date moves)
- **BC Parks:** opens ~4 months before the arrival date
- **Recreation.gov:** opens ~6 months before the arrival date *(verify per site)*
- Everything else above opens on a fixed calendar date and is fine for any June departure.
