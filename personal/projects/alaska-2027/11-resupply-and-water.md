# Resupply & Water

Correction: Whitehorse was labeled the only "resupply day" because it's the only big
city on the Alaska Highway between Grande Prairie and Fairbanks where it's worth
**spending a stay-day on errands** (groceries, laundry, propane, hardware, parts).
Groceries and water happen continuously en route. This file makes that explicit.

## Tanks (verify yours — documented 1986 Sunrader spec)
~21 gal fresh · ~18 gal gray · ~9 gal black.

Solo, conserving (navy showers, no daily full showers): ~4 gal/day fresh.
**Gray is the limiter, not fresh.** Cycle ≈ every 4–5 days: dump + fill.

## "Drive empty, fill ahead"
Water is 8.3 lb/gal. Full fresh ≈ 175 lbs; plus full gray/black ≈ another 225.
Driving with a ~5 gal reserve instead of full saves ~130 lbs on fresh alone.

**Rules**
1. **Dump every time you pass a dump**, whether you need to or not. Never drive full gray/black.
2. **Carry ~5 gal reserve** normally.
3. **Fill at the last reliable potable source before a no-water night** — within ~a day.
4. Arrive full for dry camps; arrive empty for hookup sites.

## Where water and dumps actually are
| Source | Potable fill? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Private RV parks | ✓ | Fill + dump even if not staying, usually for a fee |
| Fuel stations, Alaska Hwy | ✓ often | Many offer fill/dump free with fuel or for a few dollars |
| Municipal sani stations | ✓ | Towns along the route |
| Fred Meyer (Fairbanks, Anchorage, Soldotna) | ✓ | Historically free dump + water — verify 2027 |
| NPS (Riley Creek at Denali) | ✓ | |
| BC Provincial Parks | Some | Taps at larger parks |
| **Yukon Gov't campgrounds** | ⚠ | Hand pumps / wells, often **boil or treat**. Can't fill a tank directly — needs jugs + treatment. Fine for dishes. |
