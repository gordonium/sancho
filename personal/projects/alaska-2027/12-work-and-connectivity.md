# Work & Connectivity

**Working throughout:** 10–20 hrs/week typical, rarely over 30. Zoom calls + other tasks.
This is a scheduling input on par with the aurora block.

## Rhythm
- **Drive days = no calls.** Calls go on stay days.
- Async work fits evenings — in the north in June/July it's light until near midnight.
- Calls are anchored to other people's business hours, so they're the constraint, not total hours.

## Capacity check (front half)
61 days ≈ ~27 drive days + ~34 stay days. Blackout stay days (no calls possible) ≈ 8–10:
| Blackout | Days |
|---|---|
| Kesugi Ridge trek | 3–4 |
| Kenai Fjords all-day boat | 1 |
| Katmai floatplane | 1 |
| Talkeetna flightseeing (weather-flexible, may shift) | 1 |
| Denali bus day | 1 |
| Top of the World day (border hours, gravel) | 1 |

**~24–26 usable stay days over ~9 weeks ≈ 3/week.**
- 10–20 hrs/week: **workable.**
- 30-hr weeks: **tight** — requires evenings or an added work-anchor day.

**Recommendation:** budget **+4–6 dedicated work days** across the trip in the
tour-heavy weeks (Kenai/Homer, Denali/Talkeetna). This feeds straight into the
departure dial in `04-route-master.md`: full scope moves from ~Jun 4 to ~May 30–Jun 1,
or absorb it in evenings, or trim one content block.

## ⚠ Time zones — the calls get early
| Region | Summer offset | vs. Loveland (MDT) |
|---|---|---|
| Loveland / Alberta | UTC−6 | — |
| Saskatchewan (no DST) | UTC−6 | same |
| NE BC (Dawson Creek, Fort Nelson) — no DST | UTC−7 | −1 hr |
| Yukon (permanent) | UTC−7 | −1 hr |
| **Alaska** | **UTC−8** | **−2 hr** |
| Washington | UTC−7 | −1 hr |

If calls are anchored to Central or Eastern time: a 9 am Central call is **6 am in Alaska**;
9 am Eastern is **5 am.** Plan mornings accordingly.

### ⚠ Aurora block conflict, Aug 5–10
Up until 1–2 am nightly, in Yukon/BC time (UTC−7). A 9 am Central call there is 7 am.
**No morning calls Aug 5–10 — afternoons only.** Block it on the work calendar now.

## Connectivity
- **Starlink (unmounted, stationary)** is the primary. The enemy is **boreal forest canopy**
  at campgrounds, not coverage. Starlink needs a wide patch of open sky.
  → **Run the Starlink app's obstruction check before committing to a site.**
  → Favor lakeshores, gravel bars, open pull-throughs, river flats.
- ⚠ **Starlink roaming abroad limit.** Roam plans have historically allowed use in
  another country for about two months before requiring an address change. Canada time
  on this route totals roughly 35–40 days — under that — but **verify current policy**.
- **Cell backup:** Yukon/northern BC coverage is mostly *only in towns*; WeBoost extends
  that near towns. Confirm your US carrier's Canada roaming terms.
- **Power:** Starlink ~50–75 W (Standard) or ~25–40 W (Mini) + laptop ~40 W ≈ ~100 W.
  Four hours ≈ 400 Wh ≈ 15% of the bank. Trivial on 600 W solar.

# Session 8 — call pattern known only roughly
Exact times can't be known this far out (one standing call moved this week). Known shape:
**mostly morning to midday, plus one that's always end of day.**

## What that means up north
Times depend on the teams' time zone. **If they're on Central time:**
| Call (Central) | Yukon / NE BC / WA | Alaska |
|---|---|---|
| 9 am | 7 am | **6 am** |
| Noon | 10 am | 9 am |
| 5 pm | 3 pm | 2 pm |

**In Alaska, the whole call load would fall between about 6 am and 2 pm local.** That's
convenient: calls are done before the afternoon and the long light evenings are free.
The price is early mornings — hardest during the aurora week, with 1–2 am nights.

## Booking approach, since the calls will move
1. **Hold off on tour dates until ~6–8 weeks out**, when the call schedule is known —
   *except* things that sell out early (Katmai, Kenai Fjords peak dates). Book those early.
2. **Choose operators with flexible date changes** for anything booked early.
3. **Put all-day tours on the weekday with the fewest standing calls.**
4. **The end-of-day call is the one to watch** on tour days — all-day boats and the Katmai
   floatplane return late afternoon.

# Session 9 — calls are a light constraint
- **6 am calls: no problem.**
- **The end-of-day call is monthly** → only **2–3 during the whole trip.**
- **Whole days with no calls are common.**

Net: calls barely shape the itinerary. Revised booking rule:
- Book tours whenever convenient; the chance a tour lands on a call is low.
- Before booking an all-day tour, just check it isn't the day of the monthly end-of-day call.
- The Kesugi Sampler is one night, so it fits in a call-free stretch easily.
