## What happened
- Gordon: letters get automatic revision numbers and dates plus an optional short description; the client is never sent the Google Docs link, only a fresh PDF each time, and the PDF file name carries the revision number and date. [rec_4b51249c15 00:00:00–00:00:28]
- Peter: clients sometimes say "you spelled my name wrong"; Gordon: that's a Resend, which repackages and sends again with no new revision number (typos, a new email address, "put it at the top of my inbox"). [rec_4b51249c15 00:01:00–00:01:32]
- A second box on the file: today's date, the next revision number, a field for the reason, and a "Publish new revision" button that snapshots the letter. Publishing is separate from sending; Peter wants sending to stay intentional. [rec_4b51249c15 00:01:35–00:02:26]
- Invoices: "Mark paid" sends the paid copy as a receipt, but only after a dialog asks whether to send it, in case of a wrong click; a "Resend invoice" button resends whatever state the invoice is in (open or paid). Peter: he doesn't hand out paper receipts, he writes "paid"; receipts are worth the step. Gordon: someone (Maria Pia or Peter) then has to mark payments in the system as they reconcile, one added human step that is worth it. [rec_4b51249c15 00:02:28–00:05:20]

## Decisions
- Letter revisions numbered and dated automatically with an optional reason; client gets the PDF only, file name with revision and date. [rec_4b51249c15 00:00:00]
- Two actions: Resend (no new revision) and Publish new revision (snapshot); publishing does not send. [rec_4b51249c15 00:01:12, 00:02:19]
- Mark paid prompts before sending the receipt; Resend invoice works in any state. [rec_4b51249c15 00:04:32, 00:04:59]
