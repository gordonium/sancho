## Market and competitors
- DFW is a top-5 most competitive HVAC market; ~1,000 companies compete for AC calls; PE-backed firms outspend 10:1 on Google in normal weather but cannot handle volume in extreme heat [v2:work/clients/comfort-masters-dfw.md:44,45,214,463].
- Industry trough after the COVID replacement boom; tariffs compounding; Todd Lyles confirms the industry is "way down" [v2:work/clients/comfort-masters-dfw.md:46,107,312].
- Comfort Experts (longest-standing, 4.8 stars / 2,000 reviews, restarted its Google listing to shed reviews; ChatGPT defaults to it on vague "Comfort" queries) and Comfort Force are the other "comfort" brands in DFW; another Comfort Masters exists in Minnesota [v2:work/clients/comfort-masters-dfw.md:258,453,495].
- Service area: Fort Worth, Tarrant County, DFW; does not service east of Highway 360; does not do water heaters [v2:work/clients/comfort-masters-dfw.md:404,408,518].
- Seasonality: AC demand from ~March, furnace from November; transition slow period March to mid-April [v2:work/clients/comfort-masters-dfw.md:459].
- Stack: Service Titan with Marketing Pro, Dialpad VoIP, CallRail tracking numbers, Press Managed hosting, Cloudflare DNS [v2:work/clients/comfort-masters-dfw.md:30,31,194,202], [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/comfortmastersdfw/Billed Numbers callrail.html].
