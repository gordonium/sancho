## Voice
- Cheeky, not stuffy; professional confidence with honesty and hope; "meet them where they're at" [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:257,299-303].
- Jane's rhythm: blunt answer, then the honest qualification ("This works for a lot of people. It does not work for everybody.") [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall.md:133,134,241].
- Warmth over wit. Gordon rewrote the Olivia bio to lead with a plain admission ("I don't have that story"), call it back later, and close on the emotional beat ("that was the moment I truly fell in love with this work"), no CTA [v3:work/clients/precision-chiro/_active/olivia-bio-final.md:27-35,47-82].
- Jane's own elevator pitch is the model of concision: "I clear neural pathways. I access that system through the structure of the spine. And I do it at the most important part first." [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:334].
- Brandable chunks in use: "Not your grandpa's back cracker", "Colorado's Only Neurostructural Chiropractic Specialist", the bowling ball on a napkin ring, "whatever actually is, feels normal" (the Normative Effect of the Actual), "Don't buy raw fish at a gas station" [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:23,24,201], [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall.md:52-55,94-96].

## Rules
- Credential is written "Dr. Jane Brewer, DC, DCCJP" (no periods); DCCJP = Diplomate in Chiropractic Cranial Junction Procedures; earlier DJCC/DCJP spellings were wrong [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:41,98,230].
- Symptom pages: headline is the condition name; three bullets (empathy, professional confidence, honesty plus hope); hard limit ~6 sentences, no scrolling; CTA to the existing blog post and a condition-specific email opt-in; hero conditions first, then long tail [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:297-306].
- Bios end when the emotional arc completes; the story is the conversion [v3:work/clients/precision-chiro/_active/olivia-bio-final.md:75-82].
- Use the specific verified number when you have it (Clean and Jerk 155 lb, not "7th in the country" alone) [v3:work/clients/precision-chiro/_active/olivia-bio-final.md:84-90].
- Keep the path-to-Jane beat in Olivia's story; "never looked back" deliberately echoes Jane's long bio [v3:work/clients/precision-chiro/_active/olivia-bio-final.md:92-99].
- The splash page is locked as shipped 2026-08-26: colors, copy, layout, button wording ("Visit precisionchiroco.com →") and the byte-identical logo SVG need Gordon to re-open them [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/Precision Chiropractic/202608 - precisionchiro.com splash page/Precision Chiropractic Splash/README-BUILD.md].
- Talks and handouts: no AV, 10-15 minutes, bullets not prose, one page, keepsake not funnel; two or three nuggets that are true about the listener's own body, visualizable, consequential and repeatable at dinner [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall-prompt.md:36-52].

## Preferences
- Dashes are " - " (space hyphen space); no em dashes in client-facing copy [v3:work/clients/precision-chiro/_active/olivia-bio-final.md:103-108].
- Premium, clean round numbers in pricing ($700, not $699); golden-handshake referrals over discounts [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:142].
- Vanity domain theneckchiro.com for physical signage; logo plus domain in big readable type [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:421,434].
- Fee increases done "the quiet way": one newsletter line and a front-desk sign, no over-explaining [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:403-405].
- Jane prefers people come into the space rather than skip to a remote consult; build gentle off-ramps anyway [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:487].
- Handout symptoms are framed as signs of compensation, not a conditions-treated list [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall.md:207].

## Never
- Never write to attract patients over 65; it shapes channel choice (NPR rejected) and copy [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:155,375], [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall-prompt.md:27].
- Never imply diagnosis, claim to treat a named disease, or suggest anyone skip surgery or medical care (Colorado State Board) [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:338], [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall-prompt.md:40], [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall.md:291-295].
- No QR codes on banners ("I want it to stick to your brain") or on the handout [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-precision-chiro.md:421], [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall.md:180].
- No pitch or booking CTA in the civic talk; curiosity is the implied takeaway and is not said out loud [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall.md:172-174], [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall-prompt.md:33].
- No one-leg balance demo in a civic room (fall risk) [v2:work/clients/jane-brewer-stay-sharp-stand-tall.md:14,289].
- Don't over-author philosophical inner-voice paragraphs or close a bio with a sales line [v3:work/clients/precision-chiro/_active/olivia-bio-final.md:56,82].
