## Voice
- The One Rule: always prefer clarity over clever. Straight shooters, no fluff, no flowery pretense; if a visitor has to decode it, it is wrong [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:22].
- Three words from the customer's mouth: "Fix my heat." Every visitor arrives with a problem; the site should make them feel it is about to be solved [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:25].
- Dinner-party test: not a dinner party, a BBQ cookout after a day of fishing; earned familiarity, neighbor-casual (formality 11/10 casual), steady energy 5/10, low complexity 4/10, firmly traditional 2.5/10 [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:26,34-37].
- Elliott's reference: Dan Connor from Roseanne, flannel shirt, old Levi's, has his act together and does not need to tell you; the declarative American sentence, Hemingway without adjectives [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:56], [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:271].
- Gordon's reference: a brand-new 1970s Milwaukee living room, pride in newness with no pretension; "Welcome to Milwaukee. It ain't trying to be fancy." [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:57,58].
- The humor lives in the copy, never in the visuals: dino and T-Rex jokes stay in words; the visual metaphor is a tidy two-car garage with tools on white pegboard (a feeling, not literal imagery) [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:42].
- Unapologetic realness: "these people are showing us their real selves"; the hiring page's NO DUIs / NO VELOCIRAPTORS register is the model [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:43].
- Karen, Jeff and Tim ARE the brand (8/10 people over product); the furnace is never the hero, the person fixing it is [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:40].
- Core values: Work Hard, Work Right; Make it Beautiful; Honor the Past; Loyal to the Core [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/dmheating/WOA docs/DM Core Values.pdf], [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:37].

## Rules
- All copy on the current (pre-rebuild) site is sacrosanct and carries forward verbatim; no paraphrasing (Gordon, 2026-04-22). Protected signatures include "No matter what you need, we'll give it to you straight.", "You don't stay in business in Milwaukee for 50 years without being honest.", "We have to say these things, because not everyone is reasonable or has a sense of humor.", "The Best D&mn Heating & Cooling in Milwaukee", "Serving Southeastern Wisconsin since 1979", the "Answer the Phone" headline, the "Things we DON'T do" list with its two H2s and the weekends parenthetical, the three customer testimonials, the "Why Did We Do This?" soul block, the LifeGiver can't-do list, the oil-change "Process" block, "Giver Me the Life Giver" and its not-a-typo explanation, the "Schedule LifeGiver / BREATHE!!!" form head, and the footer identity block with the legally reviewed after-hours disclaimer [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:11-15,22-142,146-281].
- The four founding-story blocks (Karen's "He started the company in '79..." quote, the young-Jeff caption, Karen's signature, the Tim line) travel together [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:61-70]. Karen asked on 2026-06-05 to remove the line about Tim not liking pictures; treat that one block as under revision [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/dmheating/from-client/20260605 website tweaks 2026.docx].
- Writing workflow: Gordon plus Jarvis draft, Peter Nevland final edits and the final word on voice, Karen approves before anything ships; batch the whole page set to Peter rather than page-at-a-time [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:311-319].
- Jeff is not Karen's husband; Karen is married to Chris. Writing-team filter reminder [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:59], [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:25].
- One job: get the visitor to contact D&M. Phone tap and form submission are equal; no secondary CTA, no newsletter, no social follow [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:51,52], [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-content-definition.md:52-60].
- Every page ends with exactly two curated next-step links (choose-your-own-adventure) [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-content-definition.md:64-66].
- LifeGiver is for systems that are still running; repair pages for broken systems do not open with LifeGiver [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-content-definition.md:84-93].
- Speed wins (3/10 craft tradeoff): 59% mobile, CSS over images, compressed photos, no JavaScript that is not earning its keep [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:50].
- Let long copy breathe; never compress Peter's copy into accordions or tabs; lead with short, expand into long. Exception: the hiring page is a deliberate brick wall of text as a filter [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:47].
- Photography: candid, lightly styled, documentary; warm with a whisper of sepia; web-first composition with negative space and multiple framings per scene; never "hold still and smile" [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:41], [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-photo-brief.md:8-22].
- Carrier history: the company did not start as a Carrier dealer (it was Bryant); Karen wants honest wording on that claim [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/dmheating/from-client/20260605 website tweaks 2026.docx].
- Filter advice: MERV 8 is the standing recommendation for Milwaukee ductwork, MERV 11 the ceiling; high-MERV filters cause airflow problems; wording to be checked with Jeff [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:319], [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-drafts/dm-heating-faqs.docx], [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/dmheating/from-client/20260605 website tweaks 2026.docx].

## Preferences
- Karen responds well to being asked rather than told; "I am putty in whatever you start to do" [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:101].
- Karen is budget-conscious but invests on clear ROI; turned down an $1,800 photographer in 2024/25, then ran a three-day shoot in May 2026 [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:97], [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/dmheating/202605 Photoshoot/].
- Karen does not want online self-scheduling; the schedule triage overlay leads with a big clickable phone number, then forms [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:95,201], [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:51].
- Design: purple and light blue brand colors, chunky light-blue drop shadow on photos echoing the logo, bold chunky sans-serif headings like the truck logo (the trucks carry the better logo), a section featuring the most recent radio ad, random fishing pictures on every page as an inside joke [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:273-282].
- Evolution: never a flashy rebrand; repaint and polish the reliable stuff, year 3 should look like the same site lovingly maintained [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:59].
- Hyper-local, self-aware lines are tone gold ("Summer is short enough in southeast Wisconsin, you might as well stay cool while it lasts") [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:204-206].
- Karen likes her own photos used (her building, the guys at the outdoor unit, Tim on a boiler) over older or staged ones [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/dmheating/from-client/20260605 website tweaks 2026.docx].

## Never
- No stock photography, ever; no loud or high-energy color schemes; no generic service-card grids; no SEO fodder content; no building or truck-fleet hero shots; no competing-on-price signals; no swappable template layouts; no auto-sliding carousels [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:31].
- No T-Rex, cartoon or dinosaur imagery in the design; no pegboard patterns, tool illustrations or garage imagery [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-voice-profile.md:42].
- No Reviews page, no per-equipment sub-pages, no per-town service-area pages, no blog or content marketing, no individual brand pages (Carrier or Concorde) [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-content-definition.md:106-112].
- Never "improve", tighten or re-voice the sacrosanct lines [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:15].
- Do not forward drafts past Peter until the whole set is drafted [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:319].
- Jeff does not want the Brewers comment and Tim does not want the Bears comment on the site [doc:_CLIENTS/_Wizards of Ads CLIENTS/dmheating/from-client/20260605 website tweaks 2026.docx].
- Do not soften "Answer the Phone" as a standalone headline [v2:work/clients/dm-heating-sacrosanct-copy.md:85].
- No LinkedIn link, no 9-year-old YouTube link on GBP [v2:work/clients/dm-heating.md:176,177].
