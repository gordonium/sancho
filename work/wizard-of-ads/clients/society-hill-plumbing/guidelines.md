## Voice
- Greg does not speak in Hemingway-clipped fragments. His voice is tangential, conversational, layered: he starts a thought, loops through related context, and comes back to land the point. The earlier draft was Karen-of-D&M voice pasted onto Greg; wrong client, wrong voice [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:7-9,94].
- Story-starters: "Quick story, so...", "Here's the thing...", "Matter of fact...", "I'll tell you what, man...", "Now, mind you..." Connective tissue: "you know", "I mean", "so...", "but..." [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:13-27].
- Parable shape: specific situation, tangent, return, reaction, generalization, principle, why it matters [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:42-47].
- He gives credit and names sources ("I stole this from Horizon, but Horizon stole it from Frank Blau"); humility in real time [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:49-55].
- Numbers are exact and competitor taglines are quoted verbatim [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:67-73].
- Faith is integrated matter-of-factly, never a billboard [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:62-65].
- Customer questions become direct quoted speech inside Greg's parables ("customers would ask me 'What do I do about the mess?'") [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:85].
- Signature beats to keep: "Make a cup of coffee. Go sit outside. It's going to be okay." (even in serious damage scenarios); "after all, we're plumbers"; alliterative triplets ("fans blowing, pumps pumping, dehumidifiers dehumidifying"); parable similes in FAQs ("Water damage is like kidnapping") [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:78-84], [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:106].
- Brandable chunks: "Plumbing isn't about pipes, it's about taking care of people" (centerpiece); "We fix the drain and the mess"; "We're not perfect. We're plumbers."; "We don't service symptoms. We fix problems."; "Our workmanship warranty is forever"; "We're the crew the City calls"; "Call the kindest plumber in Philly" [v2:work/clients/society-hill.md:233-238], [v2:work/clients/society-hill-drafts/excavation-draft-v3-2026-06-11.md], [v2:work/clients/society-hill-drafts/contact-FINAL-2026-06-11.md].
- Character diamond (locked Aug 2025): North Star Blue Collar; Counter-Star Well-Read, Well-Spoken; Core Humility; Vulnerability Insecure, sees self as small [v2:work/clients/society-hill.md:185-191].

## Preferences
- Customer not business: "here's what YOU do", "you get", not "here's what WE do" [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:112].
- "We're going to" over "We get"; let pages run longer when Greg's voice is preserved, tighten at edit time [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:80], [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:96].
- Hero photos show the crew at work in a real Philly home; no Greg, no trucks; Meet Greg hero is Greg reflective in his shop, no tools, no truck [v2:work/clients/society-hill-drafts/home-page-FINAL-2026-06-11.md], [v2:work/clients/society-hill-drafts/meet-greg-FINAL-2026-06-11.md].
- Postcards: Monet art on the front with no logo or branding; offers on the back; "Slap it on your fridge" [v2:work/clients/society-hill.md:384-386].
- Lead with character, voice and promise on the home page, then services; the site is the ratification layer for relationships built by direct mail, free drain cleaning and the Happy Hill Club [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:35-44], [v2:work/clients/society-hill-content-definition.md].
- Inline-print drafts in chat as well as filing them [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:110].

## Never
- No em dashes, ever: hyphens, commas, semicolons, parentheses, or restructure [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:111].
- No sniping at competitors and no "Why Us" paired-opposite structures; Greg himself never disparages competitors [v2:work/clients/society-hill-NEXT-SESSION-PICKUP.md:113], [v2:work/clients/society-hill-greg-voice-profile.md:82-86].
- No SEO sludge, no commodity traits, no "Excellence Since [year]", no specials/coupons framing; those are the competition's signals [v2:work/clients/society-hill-content-definition.md], [v2:work/clients/society-hill.md:264,278-279].
- No big discounts for urgency, no spending beyond means for speed, no becoming the high-cost provider overnight, no trying to be who Greg is not [v2:work/clients/society-hill.md:278-279].
