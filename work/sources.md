# Where to learn about a client

In this order, cheapest and most trusted first. Every fact brought back carries its cite; nothing outside this tree is ever written to.

1. **Sancho's client folder:** `work/<business>/clients/<slug>/` (`entity.md`, `guidelines.md`, `knowledge.md`, `summaries/`, `transcripts/`). Read its INDEX first. [architecture §3]
2. **Gordon's working files:** `~/Sync/Gordonium Enterprises Sync/_CLIENTS/<client>/`: contracts, briefs, copy, assets, reports. Cite as `[doc:_CLIENTS/<client>/<file>]`. Read-only. [gordon 2026-10-01]
3. **Their website**, through Chrome (bot protection is common): what they sell, where, who they are, current offers. Cite `[web:<url> <date>]`.
4. **Time tracking:** one Google Sheet per client (through the Google Drive connector): what Gordon has actually done for them and when. Cite `[gsheet:<name> <date>]`. [gordon 2026-10-01]
5. **Wrike task history:** when read-only access exists (an ICE item today); until then, ask Gordon. [gordon 2026-10-01]
6. **Legacy evidence:** v2 `work/clients/<file>.md` and the roster extraction at `work/wizard-of-ads/clients/_roster-evidence-v2.md`; through the gate, cited `[v2:…]`, facts older than a year treated as history unless confirmed.

Stop when the question is answered; a "tell me about X" needs 1 and maybe 3; a brief before a meeting needs 1 to 4.
