## What happened
- Grayson: caching lyrics from open-source lyric databases and sharing them between users is direct infringement; user-uploaded charts and profiles are fine; registering a DMCA agent gives safe harbor. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:00:12, 00:01:07]
- Gordon's first question: how do the open-source lyric databases get away with it? Grayson: they aren't licensed, they survive by being small, free and unnoticed. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:01:42, 00:02:30]
- Publisher licensing means a minimum annual fee plus royalty reporting on lyrics stored and users, which pushes toward a paid "Tipelodeon Pro" and against the core pillar of not making money off musicians. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:03:31]
- Agreed shape: users upload their own data and only get their own data back; no license needed under safe harbor. Gordon: that gets us out the door and leaves room for a premium pass-through option later. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:04:50, 00:06:05]
- Grayson floated raising subscription and killing fees; Gordon won't tie those together or set subscription prices without a year of real fee data; Grayson: the platform makes roughly 10% of what performers make. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:07:11, 00:07:48]
- Gordon's play: run a year, gather user and song counts, then negotiate long multi-year contracts while small (the Netflix streaming precedent); possibly Kickstarter the license cost (~$12k/yr cited as an example). [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:08:42]
- Mothball the lyrics feature ("between retire in place and leave fingerprints"); Grayson may gate it under a super-user ability; statutory damages up to $150k per song, settlements $2–10k, and he has ~200 songs cached. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:10:44, 00:11:02]
- Gordon: normally he'd run under the radar, but the music industry's hammer and case law could put them out of business and into debt; not this one. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:11:59]
- The licensing moat (Ultimate Guitar did it with the majors) becomes a money play once there's revenue; no speculative spend now. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:12:35, 00:13:25]
- Ship with an in-app message: we know it's a pain, it's a licensing-money problem, it's on the roadmap; keep big-business music as the villain, which aligns with the brand. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:14:40]

## Decisions
- Ship Tipelodeon without auto-lyrics: users upload their own; register a DMCA agent; mothball or super-user-gate the cached-lyrics feature. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:04:50, 00:10:44, 00:14:25]
- No subscription or pricing decisions until a year of fee data exists. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:07:24, 00:08:22]
- Licensing deals are a post-revenue money play, long-term contracts negotiated while small. [rec_9c3c3bbece 00:13:25, 00:14:05]
