# Monthly Personal Review

First Saturday of the month. Deeper than the weekly — big picture on personal life, health, relationships, aspirations.

This skill stacks WITH `saturday-personal` — run both, don't absorb one into the other. Saturday personal covers system review + personal projects. This covers the longer arc.

---

## When This Triggers

- **First Saturday of each month** — detected during morning-startup or saturday-personal execution
- Always runs AFTER saturday-personal, not instead of it

---

## The Question to Hold

**Am I moving toward the life I actually want, or just maintaining?**

---

## What To Do

### Step 1: Read the routine file

Read `routines/monthly-personal-review.md` for the current standing items and their statuses.

### Step 2: Walk through each section

Present each section. For each item, surface what's known, what's changed, and whether it needs attention this month.

#### Health & Body
- **Sleep wind-down protocol** — Is the "half thread" problem improving? Has the Jarvis handoff ritual been helping? Any new patterns?
- **Food tracking review** — Nutritional gaps? Patterns from morning/evening check-ins?
- **Weed accountability** — Review tracking sheet trends. Is the one-week rule being respected?
- **Mood tracking spreadsheet** — Pull trends. What is the data telling you?
- **Fitness tracker decision** — Still want one? Fitbit vs Oura Ring.
- **Diet / gut health** — Any spicy food patterns? Symptom data accumulating?

#### Mind & Growth
- **Book recommendations + reading** — Pulitzer list curated to Gordon's tastes. Roy Williams / Steven Pinker as benchmarks. Reading history from tracking spreadsheet.
- **Smart news filtering / daily briefing** — Progress on building a curated, primary-source news brief?
- **Ted Klontz / IFS work** — How is it going? What's surfacing? Next session?
- **Memory training progress** — Mini memory palace, names at pickleball, holding thoughts mid-conversation.

#### Relationships
- **Keep in Touch rotation** — Who connected this month? Who's overdue? (Marron, Gene, Allie W, Niki, Uncle Jeff, Chad, McIntosh, Shannon, Isabel, Piyar, Grayson, Kyle, Doug)
- **The Best People check** — Zac, Laura, Piyar, Chad, Grayson, Kyle. Any need attention?
- **Leah relationship threads** — Bridge abutments, liquidity event framework, anything from recent retreats or conversations?

#### Aspirations (long arc)
- **Psychedelic retreat center** — Still on the radar? Any movement?
- **Learn to sing** — Still want to? Next step?
- **TM Center** — Next to Wizard Academy on Friday Mountain, Austin. Ray Dalio connection. Previously reached out, missed the Zoom. Reach out again?

### Step 3: Pick one priority

One personal priority gets real attention this month. Let Gordon choose, or suggest one that seems ripe based on momentum or urgency.

---

## Quick-Check Mode

Touch everything, faster pace. Not every item needs action every month. The goal is visibility, not guilt.

If Gordon wants to go deeper on one section, go deeper. If he wants to skim, skim. The review exists to keep the long arc visible — not to create homework.

---

## What This Is Not

- Not a replacement for saturday-personal (that runs first, separately)
- Not a guilt trip about items that haven't moved
- Not a planning session (unless Gordon wants it to be)

It's a monthly mirror. Look at the big picture. Notice what's moving and what's stuck. Pick one thing. Move on.
